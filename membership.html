Questions

What does my membership pay for?

Organizing. Reaching cannabis consumers in every state, getting them in front of their members of Congress, and showing up in hearing rooms when a bill moves. We'll publish what we raise and how we spend it every year.

I work in cannabis. Can I join?

Yes. Membership is for people, and plenty of people who work in cannabis are consumers too. Companies can sponsor an event, but they can't buy a membership, a board seat or a position. Industry can sponsor. It can't set our positions.

Is it tax-deductible?

No. The American Cannabis Consumer Alliance is a nonprofit that lobbies for consumers, so contributions are not tax-deductible as charitable contributions.

Why pledge instead of paying now?

Our bank account is being opened, and we won't take money any other way. When it's open, we'll email every founding member a secure payment link from ACCA directly.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes. Founding membership is $1,000 at once or $100 a month for 10 months. Either way, it's lifetime membership.

What do members vote on?

Our priorities each year, and whether ACCA endorses the bills our board recommends. Results are always published.
